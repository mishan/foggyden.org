Hexline

A native Android client for Hotline-ng, the mobile-first protocol beside classic Hotline.

  • Presence that survives a dropped connection.
  • Notifications while the app is closed.
  • An identity that lives on the phone.

Free and open source (GPL-2.0-or-later), in two builds: one with nothing proprietary in it, and one for Google Play.
